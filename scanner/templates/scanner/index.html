      How FoodLens works

      One photo goes to Gemini for text extraction and translation. A separate, conservative ingredient-rule engine checks the original Dutch or English ingredient names. The AI does not choose the dietary verdict.

      Three dietary categories. One important fourth answer.

      Vegan-compatible ingredients, vegetarian but not vegan, non-vegetarian ingredient found, or uncertain. An unreadable list, an unfamiliar ingredient or an unresolved source blocks a positive result.

      Privacy by design

      The app removes image metadata before forwarding the photo. Photos and results are not saved in the app’s database or application logs. The AI provider processes your photo under its own terms; its retention and use depend on the account and service configuration.

      Saving a scan stores the text result in this browser, not your photo. Anyone using this browser profile may see saved scans. Clear them from the Saved tab. Your dietary setting is stored on this device.

      Not a certification or allergy checker

      FoodLens cannot verify processing aids, manufacturing practices, undeclared ingredients or product certifications. AI can misread text. Always compare with the original package. “No allergen statement read” never means “allergen-free.”

      Built for the first useful version

      Dutch and English packaging only. One photo per scan. Barcode databases, full on-image translation overlays and recipe recognition are not included in this version.

      Less guessing. More understanding.Label guidance, not a safety guarantee.
